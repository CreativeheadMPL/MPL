# Motion Pulse Sample Space — Artwork System

The artwork system for **Motion Pulse Sample Space** is designed around minimal, high-end editorial and cinematic record label aesthetics.

## Design Principles
1. **Abstract & Quiet**: No faces, no stock photography, no copyrighted material, no distracting elements.
2. **Strict Color Palette**: Deep matte obsidian `#080808` to `#161616`, monochrome slate, deep crimson, dark midnight navy, and subtle warm champagne `#D4C5A5`.
3. **Square Aspect Ratio**: Standard 1:1 vinyl sleeve proportion, rendering crisply on mobile screens (65–75% width) and desktop listening cards.

## Available Artwork Presets
- `default.svg` — **Motion Pulse Archive Master**: Concentric vinyl grooves with precision center label and champagne accent.
- `sample-01.svg` — **Sample 01 (Noir Geometry)**: Minimal monochrome architectural balance, hairline geometry, and champagne focal point.
- `sample-02.svg` — **Sample 02 (Crimson Pulse)**: Deep ruby/crimson eclipse gradient with subtle acoustic resonance line.
- `sample-03.svg` — **Sample 03 (Champagne Ray)**: Warm champagne gold light prism beam piercing a dark void.
- `sample-04.svg` — **Sample 04 (Midnight Horizon)**: Indigo and twilight blue horizon with celestial acoustic coordinate grid.
- `sample-05.svg` — **Sample 05 (Acoustic Monolith)**: Architectural slate monolith with rhythmic acoustic slats.

## Custom Artwork Uploads
The admin interface allows uploading custom square artwork (PNG, JPG, WEBP, SVG) which is stored securely in `/storage/artwork/` or `/public/artwork/uploads/`.
